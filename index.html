Moto Go/No-Go

Riding weather for motorcyclists, for iPhone and iPad.

Join the betaPrivacy policySupport and contact

Moto Go/No-Go answers one question: is it a good day to ride, and where? It reads the weather for you and gives each part of the day a clear call, GO, CAUTION, or NO-GO, with a score out of 10 and the reason why.

It also records rides, keeps a trip log and a journal, and holds your bikes' maintenance records and manuals.

Riding outside the United States? For now the forecast works only in the US: it uses the US National Weather Service. Everything else in the app works anywhere. If you'd use Moto Go/No-Go where you ride, write to k12techman@gmail.com and tell us which country. It helps decide what comes next.

Tools for your bike

  • Fuel range: your economy and range from your own fuel stops, and which octane your manual calls for.
  • Tire pressure check and Tire Demystifier: what your pressures read as the air warms or cools; what a tire's size means, how old it is, and a wear check.
  • Oil Analyzer: what your manual asks for against the label on a bottle, what a rating like 10W-40 means, and your manual's own oil change steps.
  • Battery Analyzer: the kinds of motorcycle battery, and a health check with a multimeter.
  • Unit converter, and a roadside card for your plate, insurance, and papers.

The tools read your own manual and explain what they find. Your manual has the final word.

Moto Go/No-Go is for general information only. It is a guide to help you decide, not a guarantee that conditions are safe. You alone decide whether and how to ride.
