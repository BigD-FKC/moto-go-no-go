Try the Moto Go/No-Go beta

Riding weather for motorcyclists, for iPhone and iPad.

Moto Go/No-Go answers one question: is it a good day to ride, and where? It reads the weather for you and gives each part of the day a clear call, GO, CAUTION, or NO-GO, with a score out of 10 and the reason why.

It also records rides, keeps a trip log and a journal, holds your bike's maintenance records and manuals, and has tools for fuel, tires, oil, and your battery.

The app is in beta: it works, it isn't finished, and what riders say about it shapes what comes next. Testing is free.

What you need

  • An iPhone or iPad with iOS 26 or later.
  • An iPhone to record rides. An iPad doesn't record them; it shows the rides you record on your iPhone, through iCloud, and does everything else.
  • TestFlight, Apple's free app for trying apps before they're released.

Join in two steps

  1. Get TestFlight. On your iPhone or iPad, install it from the App Store:
    TestFlight on the App Store
  2. Join the beta. On the same device, tap:
    Join the Moto Go/No-Go beta

Good to know
