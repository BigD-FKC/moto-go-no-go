Get help

The app's own guide answers most questions: open Settings (the gear button) → About this app. It covers the ratings, recording rides, trips, the Garage, and each utility (fuel range, the tire tools, the Oil Analyzer, the Battery Analyzer, and the rest), with a list of common questions.

Contact

For a problem, a question, or a suggestion, write to k12techman@gmail.com. It helps to say what you were doing, what you expected, which iPhone or iPad and iOS version you use, and the app's version (at the bottom of About this app).

Testing with TestFlight? You can also send feedback, with a screenshot, from the TestFlight app.

Good to know

Moto Go/No-Go is for general information only. Its Go/No-Go result is an estimate based on weather data from third-party sources, which may be delayed, incomplete, or wrong. It does not guarantee that conditions are safe. You alone decide whether and how to ride.
